A small benchmark for model monitors and effect estimates

Does a better estimate lead to a better decision?

Many methods read a model's internals to predict a risk, a hidden state, or the effect of an edit.

ObserverBench asks whether acting on those predictions leads to better decisions. Each task fixes the data, the budget, and the decision rule. You get two scores: one for prediction and one for the decisions it supports.

Mechanistic Tomography is about which measurements to take. ObserverBench tests whether the resulting estimates help.

Does accounting for interactions help?

On a copy task in Qwen2.5-7B, a predictor that includes pairs of components cuts its error by about two-thirds (0.121 → 0.040) with the same 128 measurements.

Read two numbers

Prediction quality and action quality answer different questions.

Prediction metrics ask how accurately a method ranks or predicts test cases.

Action metrics ask what happens after the decision rule uses its limited budget on that ranking.

A method can improve one and lose on the other. ObserverBench reports both.

About these results

  • Practice tasks have public answers. Use them to learn and iterate; only hidden-test submissions go on the leaderboard.
  • Each table is one task. Only planned comparisons are numbered; rows added later and the oracle are shown for reference.
  • Most results come from one model and one setting. Where a difference is within resampling noise, the table says so.
  • In the safety tasks, a review is assumed to be correct and costs a fixed amount.
  • The scripted tasks (misleading reports, relay) are simulations, not live agents.
  • APPS submissions are paused while we review a cue in that dataset.

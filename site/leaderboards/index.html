Read two numbers

Prediction quality and action quality answer different questions.

Prediction metrics ask whether an observer ranks or estimates held-out cases accurately.

Action metrics ask what happens after the fixed controller spends its limited intervention or audit budget on that ranking.

A method can improve one and lose on the other. ObserverBench reports both.
